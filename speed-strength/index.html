Why SAS

Team practice develops the team. SAS develops the athlete. Footie Fit SAS is soccer-specific speed and strength training for middle-school and high-school athletes. The Class Experience

What Happens in a SAS Class?

SAS is not a generic workout class or bootcamp. Athletes train with experienced coaches in a structured, progressive environment built for soccer performance. Each athlete receives individual coaching within a small-group setting. Classes are capped at 8 athletes so coaches can teach, correct, progress, and challenge every athlete appropriately.

  • Soccer-specific speed, agility, strength, power, and conditioning
  • Small groups capped at 8 athletes
  • Training adjusted for age, experience, and movement ability
  • Progressive programming instead of random workouts
  • Performance testing and retesting to measure improvement

Parent FAQ

Questions Before You Claim Your Free Class

Who is SAS for?

Middle school and high school soccer athletes who want to develop physically outside of club practice. Athletes come from every club and every level — from players building a first athletic foundation to those preparing for college soccer. No prior weight room experience is required.

Is the first class really free?

Yes. New athletes can attend one SAS class at no cost to experience the coaching, training environment, and class format before starting a membership.

Are middle school and high school athletes together?

Yes, for now sessions blend middle school and high school athletes. Because groups are capped at eight, coaches individualize the work: exercises, loading, and intensity are matched to each athlete's development level rather than the group average.

What is the performance assessment?

Once your athlete joins as a SAS member, we build a performance baseline covering areas such as vertical jump and reactive strength, 10m and 30m sprints, change-of-direction and reactive agility testing, and a conditioning test. It's a membership onboarding tool — not a requirement to attend your free trial class — and we retest regularly so you can see real progress.

Does my athlete need lifting experience?

No. Strength work is coached and age-appropriate from day one. Coaches teach proper mechanics and progress loading gradually, so athletes with no weight room background train safely alongside more experienced athletes.

How is progress tracked?

Assessment results are recorded for every member and reassessed on a regular rhythm. Retest numbers are compared to the baseline, and progress is shared through Footie Fit+ reporting so you can see how your athlete is developing over time.

How many athletes are in each class?

A maximum of eight athletes per session. That cap protects coaching quality and keeps the work individualized.

Who coaches the sessions?

Coach Carol leads Monday, Tuesday, and Saturday sessions. Coach Jamie leads Wednesday and Thursday sessions. Both coach the same progressive, soccer-specific program, so athletes can attend the times that fit their schedule.

What should my athlete bring?

Training shoes, water, and comfortable training clothes. Cleats and turf shoes are useful when field work is on the plan — we will confirm what to bring before the free trial class.

Can I book a detailed performance assessment instead of the free class?

If you'd prefer a detailed assessment before your athlete starts training, contact us at info@footiefit.us and we'll schedule one. Most families start with the free class first and complete the baseline assessment when they join as a member.

How do we get started?

Reserve your athlete's first SAS class free using the form below. A coach will follow up to confirm the best class time, and after the trial we'll recommend the right membership option for your athlete.
